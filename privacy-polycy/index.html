Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 5, 2026

Overview

Finly is a personal finance tracking application designed with your privacy as a top priority. This privacy policy explains how Finly handles your data.

Key Point: Finly stores all your data locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal or financial information on external servers.

Information We Do NOT Collect

Finly does not collect, access, or share any of the following:

  • Personal identification information
  • Financial account credentials or bank information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Contact information

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Finly stores the following information locally on your device to provide its functionality:

  • Transaction Data: Transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, and categories that you manually enter
  • Categories: Custom categories you create for organizing transactions
  • App Preferences: Your chosen currency, theme, language, and notification settings

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it using the backup feature.

Backup and Export

Finly provides a backup feature that allows you to export your data as a JSON file. This file is saved to a location you choose on your device.

You have full control over this exported file. Finly does not automatically upload, sync, or transmit this data anywhere.

Notifications and Reminders

If you enable daily reminders, Finly uses your device's local notification system. These notifications are processed entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers or data transmission.

Third-Party Services

Finly does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. The app operates entirely offline and does not make network requests for data collection purposes.

If you purchase the premium version through Google Play, Google processes the payment. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle payment data.

Data Security

Your data is stored securely on your device using Android's standard data storage mechanisms. To protect your data:

  • Use your device's built-in security features (PIN, fingerprint, face unlock)
  • Keep your device's operating system updated
  • Regularly backup your data using the app's export feature

Children's Privacy

Finly does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app stores data locally on the device and does not transmit any data to external servers, making it safe for users of all ages.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it:

  • Access: View your data anytime within the app
  • Export: Export all your data using the backup feature
  • Delete: Delete individual transactions or uninstall the app to remove all data

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
